\subsection*{Influence of the component names}

To test whether the component names themselves influence the judgements, the
statements of each case were presented under the names of the other two components.
Only the component names and the words that depend on them grammatically were
exchanged; the statements were otherwise identical, character by character. Together
with the original assignment, two rotations present each set of statements once
under each of the three names (a Latin square). Each of the \resval{ts-n-models}
models rated every combination in \resval{ts-runs} runs (\resval{ts-condition},
temperature \resval{ts-temperature}). The original assignment was rerun in the same
session, so that all combinations were collected under the same conditions. Of the
\resval{ts-total} responses, \resval{ts-invalid} were invalid and are not included.

Table~\ref{tab:termswap-distance} gives the distance between the mean weight vector
of a set of statements under an exchanged name and under its original name, and
Table~\ref{tab:termswap-weights} the underlying mean weights. As a reference, the
rerun of the original lies at a distance of \resval{ts-ref-min.models} to
\resval{ts-ref-max.models} from the earlier run in Table~\ref{tab:weights-spread},
although prompts, model versions and settings were identical. Under an exchanged
name, the distance ranges from \resval{ts-dist-min.gpt-5-4-mini-2026-03-17} to
\resval{ts-dist-max.gpt-5-4-mini-2026-03-17} for gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17,
\resval{ts-dist-min.claude-haiku-4-5-20251001} to
\resval{ts-dist-max.claude-haiku-4-5-20251001} for claude-haiku-4-5-20251001 and
\resval{ts-dist-min.mistral-small-2603} to \resval{ts-dist-max.mistral-small-2603}
for mistral-small-2603.
